Peaking AI is a high-performance AI platform designed for developers building intelligent applications. Our models are optimized for speed, accuracy, and reliability at scale.

## Two ways to build

Peaking AI offers two primary interfaces depending on your use case:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="REST API" icon="rocket">
    Direct model access via HTTP. Full control over prompts, parameters, and response handling. Best for custom integrations and fine-grained workflows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Managed Agents" icon="puzzle">
    Pre-built agent infrastructure that handles planning, tool use, and multi-step reasoning. Best for complex tasks that require autonomous execution.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Platform architecture

The Peaking AI platform consists of three layers:

1. **Model Layer** — The foundation. A family of models ranging from ultra-fast (Peak-1 Flash) to deeply intelligent (Peak-1 Pro), each optimized for specific workloads.

2. **Orchestration Layer** — Agents and Workflows that compose multiple model calls into coherent, multi-step operations with built-in error handling and monitoring.

3. **Infrastructure Layer** — Global edge network, automatic scaling, request routing, and observability. You focus on your application; we handle the rest.
